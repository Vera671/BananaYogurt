# Course Syllabus – Generative AI Literacy and Applications

Generative AI Literacy and Applications is a practical course on how generative models work and how to build with them: large language models, embeddings, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and real-world applications. Classes meet on Fridays from 13:00 to 16:00.

## Grading
Assignments 30%, midterm exam 20%, final project 35%, participation and attendance 15%. Students must attend at least 80% of sessions. Attendance is recorded in GenieAi with a check-in code that the professor shows in class.

## Policies
Late work loses 10% per day for up to 3 days, after which it is not accepted. AI tools such as chatbots may be used for assignments as long as you disclose how you used them. Questions can be asked in class, in office hours, or anonymously through the Anonymous Q&A board in GenieAi.
