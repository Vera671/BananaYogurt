# Week 1: Introduction to Generative AI

Generative AI refers to models that create new content such as text, images, audio and code by learning the patterns in their training data. Discriminative models learn to classify or label inputs, while generative models learn the data distribution and sample new examples from it.

## Model families
Autoregressive language models (GPT-style) predict the next token. Diffusion models generate images by gradually removing noise. GANs train a generator against a discriminator. Variational autoencoders (VAEs) learn a compressed latent space and decode samples from it.
